Shirendel — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 21, 2026

Shirendel (“the app”) is a dhikr (tasbih) counter. This policy explains what data is processed when you use the app.

1. Data we collect

The app does not send your personal account or email to our servers. The following is stored only on your device:

  • Dhikr counters you create, count values, targets, and notes
  • Language, theme, haptics, background, and lock mode settings
  • Statistics and achievement badge progress

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app may display Google AdMob banner ads. Google may process data such as advertising IDs and device information according to its own policies.

Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

3. Data sharing

Your dhikr data is not sold or shared with third parties. The ad network (Google) processes its own data only to serve ads.

4. Storage and deletion

Data remains on your device. If you uninstall the app or use “Clear all data” inside the app, local data is deleted.

5. Children

The app is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
